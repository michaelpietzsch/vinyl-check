vinyl-check design system

Preview of the design system: icons, colours, typography, components and screen sketches. Black and anthracite are the main colours (dark is the main mood, light the variant), and red is reserved for the primary action and the recording state. Source: docs/design-system.md, icons in vinylcheck/static/icons.svg.

0 · Brand

Wordmark and signet are drawn with the same tool as the pictograms: monoline, stroke 3 on 24 units, square ends, only lines, circles and arcs. The signet is a record with a tonearm; the red dot is the label and the only colour. Files: vinylcheck/static/brand/.

2 · Colours

Roles, not decoration. Neutrals without a colour cast: black, anthracite, grey. Red belongs only to the primary action and the recording state; the right channel is violet so that red is never ambiguous. Text ≥ 4.5 : 1, icons and bars ≥ 3 : 1 in both themes.
